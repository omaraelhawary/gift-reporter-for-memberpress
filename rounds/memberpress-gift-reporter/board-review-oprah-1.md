# Board Review #1 — Audience & Accessibility (Oprah Winfrey)

**Gift Reporter for MemberPress v1.7.0**

## Verdict

MemberPress admins **will care** — unclaimed gifts mean lost revenue. They may not **get it in 10 seconds**; the story sits under filters and developer language.

## What connects

- Honest “independent, not official MemberPress” positioning in readme and notices.
- `reminder-email.php` sounds human (“Just a friendly reminder… hasn’t redeemed it yet”).
- Smart empty states (filtered vs. no data) and presets like “Unclaimed > 7 days.”
- Test email and reset template reduce send-anxiety.

## What doesn’t (yet)

- Descriptions lead with “linkage between gift givers and recipients” — lead with unclaimed gifts and follow-up.
- First visit: ten filters, wide table, no “start here” for overwhelmed site owners.
- Reminders tab: `{$product_name}` and cron talk intimidate; reminders go to **gifter** not recipient — say that plainly.

## Messaging

WordPress.org readme is clear but feature-heavy. Plugins screen is technical. Admin report is capable; reminder email is the warmest voice.

## One recommendation

One **welcome line** atop Gift Report: purpose in one sentence, then *View unclaimed*, *Set up reminders*, *Export CSV* — the cold open before the filters.
