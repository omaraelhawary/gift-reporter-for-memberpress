# Board Review #1 — Warren Buffett (Business & Economics)

**Subject:** Gift Reporter for MemberPress v1.7.0 · WordPress.org · GPL · Independent of Caseproof/MemberPress

## Verdict

**Today this is a well-built hobby with option value, not a business.** Revenue is zero; WordPress.org shows fewer than 10 active installs and zero reviews. You have invested like an owner (1.0→1.7, reminders, bulk ops, weekly summary, REST, tests) but the market has not voted with installs or dollars.

## Revenue model

- **How money is made:** It isn’t. Free/GPL on WordPress.org; no Pro tier, hosted service, or support SKU in the repo.
- **Indirect value only:** Portfolio, credibility with MemberPress shops, possible consulting—none modeled or captured in-product.
- **Ecosystem tax:** Every user must already pay MemberPress + **Gifting (Scale plan)**. You sell shovels in a fenced orchard you don’t own.

## Unit economics

- **Developer CAC:** High—years of maintenance, cron/email edge cases, WP compatibility churn—for **$0 LTV** per install.
- **Site-owner value:** Real. Unclaimed gifts are stranded cash; filters, CSV, automated reminders, and weekly admin summaries save ops time and can lift redemption. Price is what you pay; value is what they get—and they get a lot for free.
- **Support:** WordPress.org forums + email scale poorly if installs grow; no revenue to hire help.

## Pricing

- **Current price ($0):** Maximizes adoption in theory; in practice adoption is negligible—so you are not buying market share, you are donating margin.
- **Value anchor:** Comparable “ops + retention” tools in WP often sit at **$49–$149/year** for a single-site add-on. GPL does not forbid a commercial **Pro** build on your own site (support, multisite, advanced schedules, Slack/webhooks)—only the org-distributed free core.
- **Leaving money on the table** is less timidity than **no monetization architecture**.

## Competitive moat

| Factor | Durability |
|--------|------------|
| First plugin in “MP Gifting reporting” niche | Low until MemberPress notices |
| Feature depth (10 filters, bulk, cron, REST) | Medium—copyable under GPL |
| Brand / WordPress.org reviews | **Not yet built** (0 reviews) |
| Official MemberPress roadmap | **Existential risk**—Gifting docs already cover admin gift views and reminders |
| Switching costs | Low—reporting is not the membership ledger |

**Moat in one sentence:** Execution and focus, not a legal or network moat. A head start, not a castle.

## Scalability

- **Software:** Excellent marginal economics—runs on customer infrastructure; no SaaS COGS.
- **Market:** Poor—TAM = MemberPress × Gifting × admins who care about gift redemption analytics. Small pond; one vendor (Caseproof) controls the dam.
- **Growth without leverage:** More features on a free plugin **worsens** the ratio of engineering to dollars unless distribution changes.

## Business or hobby?

**Hobby with professional craftsmanship** unless you add a deliberate monetization or distribution path within 12 months. The product passes the “would I use this?” test; it fails the “does it compound cash?” test.

## One actionable recommendation

**Stop widening the free surface area.** Ship a **paid Pro** (or “Support + priority”) on your own site—e.g. multisite, export scheduling, webhook/Slack alerts, or white-glove setup—and use WordPress.org only as the top of the funnel. Measure success in **paid sites and renewal**, not version numbers. If Caseproof won’t partner, you need a revenue line before they ship “good enough” reporting inside Gifting.
